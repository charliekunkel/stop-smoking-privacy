The short version

Stop Smoking keeps everything on your phone. There is no account, no server, and no analytics. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your information.

What the app stores on your device

All of the following is saved in the app's private storage on your phone and is never transmitted anywhere:

  • Your quit plan — target quit date, cigarettes per day, cost per pack, waking hours, and alert timing preferences.
  • Your smoking history — the date and time of each cigarette you log, daily totals, and the money-saved and money-spent figures calculated from them.
  • Achievements you have earned.
  • Accountability contacts — for any contact you choose to add as a buddy, the app saves that person's name and phone number. Contacts you do not select are not saved.
  • Media you add — photos you pick as background images, and any sounds you record or upload for alerts, are copied into the app's own storage.

Permissions and why the app asks for them

PermissionWhy
NotificationsTo deliver your smoke-window alerts, health facts, end-of-day check-in, and achievements.
Alarms & remindersTo make alerts arrive at the right minute. Without it Android delays them by several minutes.
MicrophoneOnly while you are recording a custom alert sound. Recording is started by you and never happens in the background.
Photos / filesOnly to read the specific image or audio file you pick.
ContactsOnly to let you choose accountability buddies. Only the buddies you pick are saved.
Ignore battery optimisationOptional. Some phones cancel scheduled alarms to save power; this keeps alerts arriving.
Run at startupTo restore your scheduled alerts after the phone reboots, since Android clears them.

Sounds you record are placed in your phone's Notifications folder

So that Android can play your custom sound when the app is closed, a copy of each sound you record or upload for an alert is placed in your device's shared Notifications folder. This makes it selectable as a system notification sound and visible to other apps on your device that can browse audio files. It is not uploaded anywhere. Deleting the sound in the app removes that copy.

The sound that plays when you log a cigarette is played by the app itself and is not copied to shared storage.

Calls and text messages

The app never places a call or sends a message by itself. Tapping a buddy's phone number opens your dialer with the number filled in, and the relapse support feature opens your messaging app with a message drafted. In both cases nothing is sent until you choose to send it.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking, telemetry, or crash reporting.
  • No user accounts and no cloud storage.
  • Your information is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your information stays on your device until you remove it. You can erase all of it at any time by using Start Over in the app's settings, or by uninstalling the app, which deletes its private storage. Sounds you added to the shared Notifications folder are removed when you delete them in the app; any that remain after uninstalling can be deleted with your phone's file manager.

Children

Stop Smoking is intended for adults who smoke. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Health information

The app records information about your smoking that many people consider sensitive. It stays on your device. Stop Smoking is a self-help tool, not a medical device, and it does not provide medical advice.
